You sign this agreement once, by commenting on your first pull request as the CLA Assistant bot asks. It then covers all your Contributions to the Project.

"Submit" means any form of electronic, verbal or written communication sent to the Owner or the Project, including pull requests, issues, comments, and messages on the Project's communication channels, that is meant to discuss or improve the Project. It excludes communication that You conspicuously mark in writing as "Not a Contribution".
